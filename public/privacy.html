Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Overview

Danfolio Logistics provides campus keke and shuttle booking through danfolio-logistics.vercel.app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the service, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Danfolio Logistics ("Danfolio", "we") runs a campus transport app connecting students with Keke riders and shuttles.

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, phone number, role (student, rider, admin).
  • Rider details: vehicle information and identity details needed for verification.
  • Location: your GPS position while you use the app, and rider location during active trips.
  • Rides and payments: bookings, trip history, payment status and references. Card details are handled by Paystack and never stored by us.
  • Ratings, reviews and support reports.
  • Device and login information for security.

Why we use it

To match rides, process payments and refunds, show live tracking to the paid student and assigned rider only, keep accounts secure, prevent fraud, and improve the service.

Who sees it

  • Riders see only what they need for their assigned trips.
  • Students see only their own records and their assigned rider's live location after payment.
  • Admins see data needed to run and secure the service.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), Paystack (payments).

We do not sell your data.

Location

We use GPS only for the service. Live location is shared only during an active paid ride. Location history is kept for 30 days, then deleted or anonymized.

Retention

Ride and payment records are kept for the period required by law for legal and financial reasons, even if your account is deactivated. Other data is deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights (NDPA 2023)

You can request access, correction, deletion (where the law allows) or objection to processing. Contact support@danfolio.app. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit and audit logs. No system is 100% secure. Report issues to support@danfolio.app.

Age

Danfolio is for users 18 and older, or students with appropriate consent where required.

Changes

We will post updates here and notify you of major changes.
